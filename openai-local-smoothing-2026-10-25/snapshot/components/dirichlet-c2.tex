Let $m\ge1$ and let $\widetilde\Omega$ be the flat product of a torus of dimension $2m$ and a bounded smooth domain in $\mathbb R^{2m}$, with compact closure, with real coordinates grouped in quaternionic quadruples. Index the slots by $(e_0,e_1,e_2,e_3)=(1,\mathbf i,\mathbf j,\mathbf k)$. The torus directions occupy slots $0$ and $3$, along $e_0=1$ and $e_3=\mathbf k$, and the bounded domain occupies slots $1$ and $2$. For real slot derivatives $\partial_{a\alpha}$, set
\[
G_a(v)=\sum_{\alpha=0}^3e_\alpha\partial_{a\alpha}v,\qquad
H_{ab}(v)=\sum_{\alpha,\beta=0}^3e_\alpha\bar e_\beta\,\partial_{a\alpha}\partial_{b\beta}v.
\]
Let $r$ be a smooth defining function, negative inside, with nonzero boundary gradient and $H(r)>0$ on the closure. Let $B_0$ be smooth with $H(B_0)\ge0$, and let $g$ be smooth with $g>\det H(B_0)$ there. Use the Moore determinant and set
\[
g_t=(1-t)\det H(B_0+r)+t g,\qquad 0\le t\le1 .
\]
There are $b\ge b'>0$ and a constant $C$, depending only on the fixed domain and the data $r,B_0,g$, such that any smooth solution $\psi=B_0+u$ of
\[
H(\psi)>0,\qquad \det H(\psi)=g_t,\qquad u|_{\partial\widetilde\Omega}=0
\]
with positivity up to the boundary satisfies
\[
br\le u\le b'r,\qquad \|\psi\|_{C^2(\overline{\widetilde\Omega})}\le C .
\]
All three constants are independent of $t$.
