Let $d\geq1$, $p_c=2(d+1)/d$, and $I\subset\mathbb R$ be a compact interval. Define $e^{\mathrm i t\Delta}$ as the Fourier multiplier $e^{-\mathrm i t|\xi|^2}$ on $\mathbb R^d$. For $N\geq2$ and Schwartz $f$ with $\operatorname{supp}\widehat f\subset B(0,N)$,
\[
\|e^{\mathrm i t\Delta}f\|_{L^{p_c}(\mathbb R^d\times I)}
 \lesssim_{d,I}[\log(2+N)]^{2/p_c}\|f\|_{L^{p_c}(\mathbb R^d)}.
\]
