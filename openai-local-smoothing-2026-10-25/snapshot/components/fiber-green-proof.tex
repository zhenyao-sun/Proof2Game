Define $\mathcal Lw=M:H(w)=\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}(MH(w))$. The slot formulas give $\mathcal Lw=M^\mathbb R:D^2w$. The column divergence condition gives $\mathcal Lw=\operatorname{div}(M^\mathbb R\nabla w)$. Since $\eta$ depends only on $z$, $\mathcal L\eta=M_{TT}\Delta_z\eta$. Green's identity gives
\[
\int_\Omega u\mathcal L\eta-\eta\mathcal Lu
=-\int_{\partial_{\rm lat}\Omega}\eta(\nu^*M\nu)\partial_{\mathbf n}u\,dS,
\quad
\mathbf n=\nabla r/|\nabla r|,\quad \nu=G(r)/|\nabla r|.
\]
The term with $u$ on the wall vanishes, and the compact support of $\eta$ eliminates parameter-end terms. On the wall $\nabla u=(\partial_{\mathbf n}u)\mathbf n$ since the trace is zero. Smooth division gives $u=r\rho$ near the wall, so $\partial_{\mathbf n}u=\rho|\nabla r|$. The coarea formula on the total space rewrites $dS/|\nabla r|$ as $\delta(r)\,dz\,d\zeta$, and coarea at fixed $z$ rewrites $\delta(r)\,d\zeta$ as $dS_{\rm fib}/|\nabla_\zeta r|$. Substitution yields the formula.
