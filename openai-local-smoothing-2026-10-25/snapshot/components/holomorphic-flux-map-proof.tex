Use the tensor $B$ of Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-2-1}, extending it by zero in the two passive directions. For a source coordinate $I$ set $Q_I=\mathcal Z_1^I+\mathbf k\mathcal Z_2^I$. The planes $\mathbb C_{\mathbf i}$ and $\mathbf k\mathbb C_{\mathbf i}$ are orthogonal for $\langle u,v\rangle=\operatorname{Re}(u\bar v)$, so $B^{IL}=\operatorname{Re}(Q_I\overline{Q_L})$. Its real and state rows are
\[
Q_{\mathsf X^\mu}=\sum_aM_{\mu a}\mathbf i G_a,\qquad
(Q_{w_{1a}},Q_{w_{2a}})=(G_a,\mathbf k G_a).
\]
Multiplication by $h=h_1+\mathbf k h_2$ on a source auxiliary pair has real rows $(h_1,-h_2)$ and $(h_2,h_1)$, which send $(G_a,\mathbf kG_a)$ to $(\bar hG_a,\mathbf k\bar hG_a)$. The derivative $\partial_{z_a}\Theta_\mu=M_{\mu a}$ follows from the symmetry of $C^\mu$. Together with $-\mathbf i h\mathbf i=\bar h$ for $h\in\mathbb C_{\mathbf k}$, this shows that the target triple of Gram rows is $Q_{\mathsf X^\mu},-\mathbf iQ_{\mathsf X^\mu},-\mathbf jQ_{\mathsf X^\mu}$, with zero contribution from the passive directions. These are the three rows $\bar e_\alpha Q_{\mathsf X^\mu}$ representing a quaternionic outer product.

The real volume Jacobian equals $J$ by holomorphicity. The pushforward as a density under the extended map (which fixes $\mathsf X$) is
\[
\widetilde B\circ\Theta=J^{-1}D\Theta\,B\,D\Theta^{\mathsf t}.
\]
The Gram rows just computed identify $\widetilde B=(\widetilde\chi\widetilde\chi^*)^{\mathbb R}$. Varying $z$ at fixed $\eta$ gives the columns of $M$, so $(\partial_\omega\eta)M=0$ and $(\partial_\omega\eta)\widetilde\chi=0$.

For each target column $K$, test the divergence against a real smooth $\varphi$ of compact support. Changing variables sends the integral to $-\sum_{I,L}\int B^{IL}(\partial_L\Theta^K)\partial_I(\varphi\circ\Theta)$. Integration by parts yields
\[
J(\operatorname{div}\widetilde B)^K\circ\Theta
=\sum_L(\partial_L\Theta^K)(\operatorname{div}B)^L
 +\sum_{I,L} B^{IL}\partial_I\partial_L\Theta^K.
\]
The first sum vanishes by Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-2-1}. In the second, both diagonal state blocks are $(\operatorname{Re}(G_a\overline{G_b}))_{ab}$, and the mixed state block vanishes. The real components of $\Theta$ satisfy $\partial_{w_{1a}}\partial_{w_{1b}}\Theta^K+\partial_{w_{2a}}\partial_{w_{2b}}\Theta^K=0$ by the Cauchy--Riemann equations. All terms involving the other coordinates vanish since they enter affinely.
