Let \(M\ge0\), \(0<b<1/4\), \(D=\log(3/b)\), and let
\(V:[b,3]\to[0,\infty)\) be continuous with
\[
 \int_b^3 s\log(s/b)V(s)\,ds\le M,\qquad
 \sup_{1/2\le s\le3/2}V(s)\le M .
\]
Let \(E\ge0\) be smooth on
\(\mathbb R_T\times\{\tau\in\mathbb R^2:b\le|\tau|\le3\}\) and
assume \(\Delta_{T,\tau}E\le V(|\tau|)E\) in the interior.
Set
\[
 H(T,s)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}
    E(T,s(\cos\theta,\sin\theta))\,d\theta .
\]
There is a positive constant \(c(M)\) such that for \(b\le r\le1\),
\[
 H(0,r)\ge c(M)\frac{\log(r/b)}{D^2}\int_1^2H(T,b)\,dT .
\]
