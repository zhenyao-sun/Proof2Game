Let \(n\ge2\), \(A\ge0\), and \(L(R)=\log(2+R)\). For \(0<\rho\le1\), let \(T_\rho(\omega,y)\) be the open \(\rho\)-neighborhood of \(y+[-1/2,1/2]\omega\) for \(\omega\in S^{n-1}\) and \(y\in\mathbb R^n\). Define
\[
 K_\rho f(\omega)=\sup_{y\in\mathbb R^n}
 \frac{1}{|T_\rho(\omega,y)|}\int_{T_\rho(\omega,y)}|f(x)|\,dx .
\]
Suppose
\(\|K_\rho f\|_{L^n(S^{n-1})}\le C L(1/\rho)^{2A}\|f\|_{L^n(\mathbb R^n)}\) for every \(0<\rho\le1\) and \(f\in L^n(\mathbb R^n)\). Let \(D_\rho f(x)\) be the supremum over \(\omega\in S^{n-1}\) of the averages of \(|f|\) over the open \(\rho\)-neighborhoods of \(\{x+u\omega:1/2\le u\le1\}\). Then
\[
 \|D_\rho f\|_{L^n(\mathbb R^n)}\lesssim L(1/\rho)^{2A}\|f\|_{L^n(\mathbb R^n)}.
\]
