Let $n\ge2$, $2\le p<\infty$, and
\[
\alpha>\max\left\{\frac12,n\left(\frac12-\frac1p\right)\right\}.
\]
For $r>0$ let $B_r^\alpha$ be the Fourier multiplier with symbol $(1-|\xi|^2/r^2)_+^\alpha$. For $f\in\mathcal S(\mathbb R^n)$ define
\[
\mathcal G_\alpha f(x)
 =\left(\int_0^\infty |r\partial_r B_r^\alpha f(x)|^2\frac{dr}{r}\right)^{1/2}.
\]
Then $\|\mathcal G_\alpha f\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)}\le C_{n,p,\alpha}\|f\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)}$, and $\mathcal G_\alpha$ extends to $L^p(\mathbb R^n)$.
