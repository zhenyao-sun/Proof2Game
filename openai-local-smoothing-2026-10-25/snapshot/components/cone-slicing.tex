Let \(n\ge2\), \(2\le b<\infty\), and \(b'\le a\le\infty\). Let \(E\) denote extension with the phase \(x\cdot\omega\) and surface measure \(d\sigma\) on \(S^{n-1}\). Assume \(E:L^a(S^{n-1})\to L^b(\mathbb R^n)\) is bounded. Then extension \(E_\Sigma\) with phase \(x\cdot\xi+t\tau\) and induced surface measure on \(\Sigma=\{(\rho\omega,\rho):1\le\rho\le2,\ \omega\in S^{n-1}\}\) is bounded \(L^a(\Sigma)\to L^b(\mathbb R^{n+1})\).
