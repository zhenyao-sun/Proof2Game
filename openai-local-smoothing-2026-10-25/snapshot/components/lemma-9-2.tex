Let \(n\ge2\), \(p=2n/(n-1)\), and \(U(T)=e^{-iT|D|}\), where \(|D|=\sqrt{-\Delta}\). Fix \(\chi\in C^\infty_c((1/2,2))\) equal to one near \(1\). Suppose there are \(A\ge0\) and \(C\) such that for all \(N\ge2\) and Schwartz \(f\),
\[
 \|U(T)\chi(|D|/N)f\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n\times[1,2])}
 \le C[\log(2+N)]^{A}\|f\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)}.
\]
With \(Eg(X)=\int_{S^{n-1}}e^{iX\cdot\omega}g(\omega)\,d\sigma(\omega)\) and \(d\sigma\) surface measure, for \(R\ge2\), \(z\in\mathbb R^n\) and \(g\in L^p(S^{n-1})\) one has
\[
 \|Eg\|_{L^p(B(z,R))}\lesssim [\log(2+R)]^A\|g\|_{L^p(S^{n-1})}.
\]
