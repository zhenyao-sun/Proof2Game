For Hermitian quaternionic $m\times m$ matrices set $A:B=\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}(AB)$. Write $\det A$ for the Moore determinant, the product of the real eigenvalues counted with quaternionic multiplicity. It is a homogeneous polynomial of degree $m$. Its cofactor $C(A)$ is the Hermitian matrix determined by
\[
C(A):B=\left.\frac{d}{dt}\det(A+tB)\right|_{t=0}.
\]
It is polynomial of degree $m-1$, with $C(A)=(\det A)A^{-1}$ for $A>0$. For $A\ge0$ one has $C(A)\ge0$, and when $A$ is also singular, $C(A)$ kills the image of $A$. On positive definite matrices $\log\det$ is concave and increasing in the matrix order.

For every Hermitian $A$, unit vector $v\in\mathbb H^m$ and real $t$,
\[
v^*C(A)v=\det(A|_{v^\perp}),\qquad
\det(A+tvv^*)=\det A+t\,v^*C(A)v,
\]
where $A|_{v^\perp}$ is compression to the quaternionic orthogonal complement and the determinant in dimension zero is one. For a Hermitian $M=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\ell&c^*\\c&D\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ with $D>0$, put $\sigma=\ell-c^*D^{-1}c$ and let $v_0$ be the first coordinate vector. Then
\[
\det M=(\det D)\sigma,\qquad M-\sigma v_0v_0^*\ge0.
\]
