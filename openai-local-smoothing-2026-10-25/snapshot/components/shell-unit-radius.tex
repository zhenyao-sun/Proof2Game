Let $n\ge2$, $2\le p<\infty$, $\sigma>\max\{0,(n-1)/2-n/p\}$ and $0<c<C<\infty$. For $0<\delta\le1/8$ and $\psi\in C_c^\infty((1/2,2))$, let $A_{\delta,r}$ have Fourier symbol $\psi((1-|\xi|/r)/\delta)$. If $g$ is Schwartz with Fourier support in $c\le|\xi|\le C$, then
\[
\left\|\sup_{1\le r\le2}|A_{\delta,r}g|\right\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)}
\le C_{c,C,n,p,\sigma,\psi}\delta^{-\sigma}\|g\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)} .
\]
The coefficient is uniform over bounded subsets of $C_c^\infty((1/2,2))$ for $\psi$.
