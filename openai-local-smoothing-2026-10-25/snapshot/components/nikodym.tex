Let \(n\ge2\), \(p_c=2n/(n-1)\), and \(0<\delta\le1\). Define \(N_\delta f(x)\) to be the supremum over all unit line segments through \(x\) of the average of \(|f|\) over the open \(\delta\)-neighborhood of the segment. For every \(f\in L^n(\mathbb R^n)\),
\[
 \|N_\delta f\|_{L^n(\mathbb R^n)}
 \lesssim_n [\log(2+1/\delta)]^{4/p_c}\|f\|_{L^n(\mathbb R^n)} .
\]
