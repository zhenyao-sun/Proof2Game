Let $U(t)=e^{-it|D|}$ on $\mathbb R^n$, $n\ge2$, where $|D|=\sqrt{-\Delta}$. Let $0<c<C<\infty$, $2\le p<\infty$ and $\sigma>\max\{0,(n-1)/2-n/p\}$. For Schwartz $g$ with $\operatorname{supp}\widehat g\subset\{c\le|\xi|\le C\}$ and $R\ge1$,
\[
\|U(t)g\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n\times\{R\le|t|\le 2R\})}
 \le C_{c,C,n,p,\sigma}R^{1/p+\sigma}\|g\|_p.
\]
Also $\sup_{|t|\le1}\|U(t)g\|_p\le C_{c,C,n}\|g\|_p$.
