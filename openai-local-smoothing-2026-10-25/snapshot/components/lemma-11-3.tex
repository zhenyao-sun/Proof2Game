Let $n\ge2$, let $H$ be a Hilbert space, $2<p<\infty$, $0<\delta\le1$, and $N=n+3$. Suppose $K:\mathbb R^n\to H$ is continuously differentiable and
\[
 \|K(x)\|_H+\|\nabla K(x)\|_{H^n}\le C_*\delta^{-N}(1+|x|)^{-N}.
\]
Write $S^{(k)}f=2^{nk}K(2^k\cdot)*f$ and let $B_q$ bound the norm of $K*$ from $L^q(\mathbb R^n)$ to $L^q(\mathbb R^n;H)$ for $q=2,p$. For each finite sequence of Schwartz functions $f_k$ indexed by integers,
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \left\|\left(\sum_k\|S^{(k)}f_k\|_H^2\right)^{1/2}\right\|_p \\
 \le C_{n,p}\left(B_2+C_*\delta+
 [1+\log(1/\delta)]^{1/2-1/p}B_p\right)
 \left\|\left(\sum_k|f_k|^2\right)^{1/2}\right\|_p.
\end{gathered}
\]
