Let \(n\ge2\), \(2\le p<\infty\), \(I=[1,2]\), and
\(s_+(p)=\max\{0,(n-1)/2-n/p\}\). With the Fourier convention
\(\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R^n}e^{-iX\cdot\xi}f(X)\,dX\) and
inverse factor \((2\pi)^{-n}\), let \(U(T)\) and \(J\) be the
multipliers \(e^{-iT|\xi|}\) and \((1+|\xi|^2)^{1/2}\), respectively.
For every \(\varepsilon>0\) and every complex Schwartz function \(f\),
\[
 \|U(T)f\|_{L^p(I\times\mathbb R^n)}
 \le C_{n,p,\varepsilon}\|J^{s_+(p)+\varepsilon}f\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)}.
\]
At \(p=2\) one can take \(\varepsilon=0\).
