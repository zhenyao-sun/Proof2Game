Let $n\ge2$, $d=n-1$, $w_0=1/10$, $a>0$, $s=|\tau|$, and $z=(T,\tau)\in\mathbb R\times\mathbb C_{\mathbf k}$ with $a<s<4$. At fixed $z$, write $X\in\mathbb R^n/(100\mathbb Z^n)$ and $(P,Z)\in\mathbb C_{\mathbf k}^d\times\mathbb C_{\mathbf k}$. On $Y\ne0$ define
\[
 Y=(\tau+Z)/\sqrt 2,\qquad W=P/Y,\qquad
 \Phi=(Z-\tau)/\sqrt 2+(P\cdot P)/(2Y),\qquad Q=\Phi/a .
\]
Here the dot denotes the complex bilinear product and the norm is Euclidean. Let $\Omega_z$ be the fiber $r<0$ in this open set, where $r=|W|^2/w_0^2+100|Q|^2-1$, and put
$\Omega=\{(z,X,P,Z):a<s<4,\ (X,P,Z)\in\Omega_z\}$. For the full operators use $n+1$ real triples $x_{j0}+x_{j1}\mathbf i+x_{j2}\mathbf j$ with real slots $(T,X)$ and auxiliary slots given by $(\tau,P,Z)\mathbf i$, in matching index order. Thus if an auxiliary coordinate is $p+\mathbf kq$, its slots are $(x_{j1},x_{j2})=(p,q)$. Write $(e_0,e_1,e_2)=(1,\mathbf i,\mathbf j)$, $\partial_{j\alpha}=\partial/\partial x_{j\alpha}$, and set
\[
G_j(v)=\sum_{\alpha=0}^2 e_\alpha\partial_{j\alpha}v,\qquad
H_{jk}(v)=\sum_{\alpha,\beta=0}^2 e_\alpha\bar e_\beta\partial_{j\alpha}\partial_{k\beta}v.
\]
The spatial operators $G_{\rm fib},H_{\rm fib}$ use only the $n$ indices belonging to $(X,P,Z)$ at fixed $(T,\tau)$. Write $\mathrm d\zeta=\mathrm dX\,\mathrm dP\,\mathrm dZ$ for ordinary fiber volume.

The fiber chart $(P,Z)\mapsto(W,Q)$ is a biholomorphism to the ellipsoid $|W|^2/w_0^2+100|Q|^2<1$ and extends to neighborhoods of the closures. Its inverse is
\[
 Y=\frac{\sqrt 2\,\tau+aQ}{1+W\cdot W/2},\qquad P=WY,\qquad Z=\sqrt 2\,Y-\tau .
\]
On the closed fibers, uniformly for $a<s<4$, one has $|Y|\asymp s$ and
\begin{align*}
 H_{\rm fib}(r)&\ge c s^{-2}I,
 &G_{\rm fib}(r)G_{\rm fib}(r)^*&\le H_{\rm fib}(r),\\
 \mathrm dP\,\mathrm dZ&\asymp a^2s^{2d}\,\mathrm dW\,\mathrm dQ,
 &\det H_{\rm fib}(r)&\asymp a^{-2}s^{-2d}.
\end{align*}
The full $H(r)$ is positive semidefinite with a one-dimensional quaternionic kernel contained in $G(r)^\perp\cap\ker H(|\Phi|^2)$. The spatial $H_{\rm fib}(|\Phi|^2)$ has rank one and nonzero eigenvalue comparable to one. The full gradient satisfies $|G(|W|^2)|\lesssim s^{-1}$.
