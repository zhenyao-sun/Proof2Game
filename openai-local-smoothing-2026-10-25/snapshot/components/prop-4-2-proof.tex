Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-4-dirichlet} gives the smooth potential, $A:B=0$, and the positivity and conservation of $A$. The Schur formula of Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-3-2} gives
\[
 H(B_0+u)=\sigma_0 e_Te_T^*+\Sigma,\qquad
 \sigma_0=\ell-c^*D_u^{-1}c,\qquad \Sigma\ge0,\qquad
 \det H(B_0+u)=\lambda\mu\sigma_0
\]
with $e_T$ the first coordinate vector. Consequently
\[
 A:H(u)=A:\Sigma+\mu\sigma_0=A:\Sigma+\lambda^{-1}\det H(B_0+u).
\]
The scalar Schur complement isolates the time entry as in the real tensor estimate of Serre \cite{r-71}, Proposition 2.2. The segment integral $E_z$ will produce a Laplacian that cancels the signed full determinant.

Apply Lemma~\ref{ms:fiber-green} on the total domain of these fibers. The inverse chart of Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-4-1} gives compact closed fibers over compact parameter sets and a nonzero spatial gradient of $r$ on the wall. Write $\nu=G(r)/|\nabla r|$ for the encoded outward unit normal.

Take $M=A$ and reverse the sign for $X_0$. The regularizing cofactor has zero normal coefficient by Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-4-dirichlet}, so the boundary term is the stated flux $\mathcal B(z)=\int\rho\,G(r)^*A_{\rm w}G(r)\delta(r)\,\mathrm d\zeta$. The interior identity gives
\[
 \Delta_z X_0(z)=\mathcal B(z)-\int_{\Omega_z} A:\Sigma\,\mathrm d\zeta
 -\lambda^{-1}\int_{\Omega_z}\det H(B_0+u)\,\mathrm d\zeta .
\]

For the second term of the energy take $M=C(H(B_0+\theta u))$, whose columns are conserved by Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-3-3}. The product rule from Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-3-1} applied to $r\rho$ on the wall shows
\[
 H(B_0+\theta u)|_{\nu^\perp}=(B+\theta\rho H(r))|_{\nu^\perp},
\]
since the two cross terms have a factor $G(r)$ and vanish on compression. The null line of $H(r)$ belongs to $\nu^\perp\cap\ker B$ by Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-4-1}; hence this compression is singular. The cofactor restriction identity in Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-3-2} gives $\nu^*M\nu=0$ without a sign assumption on $H(B_0+\theta u)$. The same identity for $e_T$ gives $M_{TT}=\det D_{\theta u}$. Integrating Green's formula in $\theta$ and dividing by $\lambda$ therefore yields
\[
 \Delta_z E_z(u)=\lambda^{-1}\int_{\Omega_z}\det H(B_0+u)\,\mathrm d\zeta .
\]
In fact $M:H(u)=\partial_\theta\det H(B_0+\theta u)$, and the lower endpoint contributes $\det B=0$ because $B$ has rank at most one by the same holomorphic Hessian formula. Summing the two Laplacian identities proves the balance in distributions. The fiber chart identifies both volume and wall integrals smoothly with integrals on fixed domains, so the identities hold pointwise. Positivity of $A$ and $\Sigma$ gives $A:\Sigma\ge0$.
