Let $k=3$ or $4$, $(e_0,e_1,e_2,e_3)=(1,\mathbf i,\mathbf j,\mathbf k)$, and let $u$ be a smooth real function of real coordinates $z_{a\alpha}$ with $1\le a\le m$ and $0\le\alpha<k$. Define $H_{ab}(u)=\sum_{\alpha,\beta}e_\alpha\bar e_\beta\,\partial_{a\alpha}\partial_{b\beta}u$, with $\partial_{a\alpha}=\partial/\partial z_{a\alpha}$. For a quaternionic Hermitian $A$, let $C(A)$ be the derivative of its Moore determinant under the real pairing $\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}(AB)$. Then
\[
\sum_{a,\alpha}\bar e_\alpha\,\partial_{a\alpha} C(H(u))_{ab}=0
\qquad(1\le b\le m).
\]
