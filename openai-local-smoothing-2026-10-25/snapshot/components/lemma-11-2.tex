Let $n\ge2$, $2<p<\infty$, $\varepsilon>0$, $\sigma=\max\{0,(n-1)/2-n/p\}+\varepsilon$ and $H=L^2([1,2],ds)$. Fix $A\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R\times(0,\infty))$ and $\varphi\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)$. For $0<\delta\le1$, let $T_\delta f(x,s)$ be the spatial Fourier multiplier $A(s,|\xi|)\varphi((s-|\xi|)/\delta)$ applied to $f$. Then
\[
\|T_\delta f\|_{L_x^p(H)}\le C\delta^{1/2-\sigma}\|f\|_p,
\qquad
\|T_\delta f\|_{L_x^2(H)}\le C\delta^{1/2}\|f\|_2,
\]
with constants independent of $\delta$.
