Let $n\ge2$, $d=n-1$, $0<h\le1$, and $0<c_0<C_0$. In any orthonormal frame $(x,b)$ on the covering space of $\mathbb T^n=\mathbb R^n/(100\mathbb Z^n)$, write $\xi=(\xi_x,\xi_b)$ and $\kappa=(\xi_b+|\xi|)/\sqrt2$. For frequencies with $\kappa>0$ put $v=\xi_x/\kappa$, and assume $c_0\le h|\xi|\le C_0$ and $|v|\le1/10$. For $y>0$ and $W\in\mathbb C_{\mathbf k}^d$ with $|W|\le1/10$, where $\mathbb C_{\mathbf k}=\mathbb R+\mathbf k\mathbb R$ and $\mathbf k^2=-1$, let
\[
 b_0=\frac{1+\operatorname{Re}(W)\cdot v/2}{\sqrt2},\qquad
 \Psi=\kappa y|W-v|^2/2,\qquad
 m_0=b_0(\kappa/y)^{d/2}e^{-\Psi}.
\]
Write $\mathsf S_0$ for the multiplier that multiplies the coefficient at $\xi$ by $m_0$. On these frequencies, $\kappa\asymp h^{-1}$, $b_0\asymp1$, and
\[
 m_0^{-1}=b_0^{-1}(y/\kappa)^{d/2}e^\Psi
     \asymp(hy)^{d/2}e^\Psi .
\]
In particular $h^d m_0\asymp1$ if $y\asymp h$.

If $f(X)=\sum_\xi c_\xi e^{\mathrm i X\cdot\xi}$ is a trigonometric polynomial on $\mathbb T^n$ supported on these frequencies, then the number of supported frequencies is $O(h^{-n})$ and $\sum_\xi|c_\xi|\lesssim h^{-n/2}\|f\|_2$. The norm uses ordinary torus volume. The estimates are uniform in the frame, with constants depending on $n,c_0,C_0$ and, for the two-sided bound when $y\asymp h$, the fixed comparison constants for $y/h$.
