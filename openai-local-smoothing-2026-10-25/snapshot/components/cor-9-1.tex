Let \(n\ge2\), \(p_c=2n/(n-1)\), and let \(d\sigma\) be surface measure on \(S^{n-1}\). Write
\[
 Eg(X)=\int_{S^{n-1}} e^{iX\cdot\omega}g(\omega)\,d\sigma(\omega).
\]
Let \(T_\delta(\omega,x)\) be the open \(\delta\)-neighborhood of \(x+[-1/2,1/2]\omega\). Define \(K_\delta f(\omega)\) as the supremum over \(x\in\mathbb R^n\) of the average of \(|f|\) over \(T_\delta(\omega,x)\). For \(R\ge2\), \(z\in\mathbb R^n\), and \(0<\delta\le1\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \|Eg\|_{L^{p_c}(B(z,R))}&\le C_n[\log(2+R)]^{2/p_c}\|g\|_{L^{p_c}(S^{n-1})},\\
 \|K_\delta f\|_{L^n(S^{n-1})}&\le C_n[\log(2/\delta)]^{4/p_c}\|f\|_{L^n(\mathbb R^n)}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Every subset of \(\mathbb R^n\) containing a unit line segment in each direction has Hausdorff dimension \(n\).
