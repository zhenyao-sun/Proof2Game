Set $U(t)=e^{-it|D|}$. With the scalar Fourier transform $\widehat\psi(t)=\int e^{-itu}\psi(u)\,du$, inversion gives
\[
 A_{\delta,r}g(x)=\frac{\delta r}{2\pi}
 \int_{\mathbb R}e^{itr}\widehat\psi(\delta rt)U(t)g(x)\,dt.
\]
The supremum for $1\le r\le2$ is bounded by $C_M\delta\int(1+\delta|t|)^{-M}|U(t)g(x)|\,dt$. On $R\le|t|\le2R$ with dyadic $R\ge1$, H\"older in time and \ref{ms:lemma-10-3} give an $L_x^p$ bound for the contribution of
\[
 C_M\delta(1+\delta R)^{-M}R^{1-1/p}R^{1/p+\sigma}\|g\|_p.
\]
For $M>1+\sigma$ its sum is $O(\delta^{-\sigma}\|g\|_p)$ by splitting at $R=\delta^{-1}$. The contribution of $|t|\le1$ is $O(\delta\|g\|_p)$ by the short-time bound in \ref{ms:lemma-10-3}.
