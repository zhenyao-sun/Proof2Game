By Proposition~\ref{ms:prop-2-2}, $A^0$ is positive semidefinite, has divergence-free real columns and satisfies $(\partial\Phi)A^0=0$. The holomorphic Hessian and gradient identities in Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-3-1} show that the images of $H(|\Phi|^2)$ and $G(g(Q))$ are contained in the quaternionic span of $(\partial\Phi)^*$. The averaged current still annihilates this span because $(\partial\Phi)^*$ is independent of $X$. Averaging preserves positivity and conservation. Multiplication by $g(Q)$ preserves positivity and adds only the term given by contraction with $G(g(Q))$ in the real column divergence, which vanishes. In particular $A_{\rm w}:H(|\Phi|^2)=0$.

By Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-4-1}, the null line of $H(r)$ is orthogonal to $G(r)$ and lies in $\ker H(|\Phi|^2)$. The cofactor has image in this line, is positive semidefinite, and has time entry $\det H_{\rm fib}(r)>0$ by Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-3-2}. Its real columns have zero divergence by Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-3-3}. This proves the matrix claims and $\mu>0$, and the spatial eigenvalue claim follows from Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-4-1}.

For the density ceiling, apply Lemma~\ref{ms:wave-cap-estimates} to the annular cap. It gives $\kappa\asymp h^{-1}$ and $\sum_\xi |\widehat f(\xi)|\lesssim h^{-n/2}$. The weight in Proposition~\ref{ms:prop-2-2} is bounded by $(\kappa/|Y|)^{d/2}\lesssim h^{-d}$ because $|Y|\asymp s>a\ge h$, and its polarization columns are bounded on these fibers. Hence $\|A^0\|\lesssim h^{-n-2d}$. The cofactor adds at most $Ch^{50n}a^{-2}s^{-2d}$ to $\mu$ by Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-4-1}, which gives $\mu^{1/d}\le h^{-30n}$ for sufficiently small $h$.

Apply Theorem~\ref{ms:thm-a-1} to the spatial variables. The defining Hessian is positive definite by Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-4-1}, the background $B_{\rm fib}$ is positive semidefinite with zero determinant since $n\ge2$, and the density $\lambda\mu$ is strictly positive. The parametrization by the fixed ellipsoid gives the smooth family of domains. The theorem gives existence, uniqueness, smoothness in the parameters, and $u\le0$ by its barriers. Finally the eigenvalues of $B_{\rm fib}+\alpha I$ are $\beta+\alpha$ and $d$ copies of $\alpha$, giving the stated determinant.
