The wave operator in $(t,x,s_0)$ is $2\partial_t\partial_{s_0}+\Delta_x$, so at frequency $\kappa$ in $s_0$ it becomes $2\mathrm i\kappa\partial_t+\Delta_x$. Substituting $T=(t-s_0)/\sqrt2$ and $b=(t+s_0)/\sqrt2$ into the phase gives the asserted mode because $|\xi|^2-\xi_b^2=|\xi_x|^2$. The first entry of $\mathcal P(\partial_{s_0}\psi,\nabla_x\psi)^{\mathsf t}$ is $(2\partial_t\partial_{s_0}+\Delta_x)\psi$ and the other entries cancel by commutation of derivatives. Evaluating $C(p)$ gives the displayed block matrix. Its quadratic form is the stated square, and its kernel consists of multiples of $E(v)$. On the mode, $\mathcal P$ acts by $\mathrm i C(p)$, yielding the vector solution.

Translation in physical coordinates by $-\mathrm i\rho(1,\omega)$, with $\rho>0$ and $|\omega|=1$, multiplies a mode by $e^{\rho(\xi\cdot\omega-|\xi|)}=e^{-\rho|\xi||\xi/|\xi|-\omega|^2/2}$. At $|\xi|\asymp N$ and $\rho N\gg1$ its angular width is of order $(\rho N)^{-1/2}$. The quadratic flux $q(w)^\mu=w^{\mathsf t}C^\mu w/2$ is $(w_0^2,w_0w',-|w'|^2/2)$ in the null frame. In physical coordinates its time entry and spatial length both equal $(w_0^2+|w'|^2/2)/\sqrt2$. At $w=E(v)$ the physical flux is $(|\xi|,\xi)/\kappa$.

In the sum of the two fluxes, symmetry cancels the mixed terms of $z^*C^\mu z$. Applying the quadratic form for $-C(p)$ to each real state gives $p\cdot q=-\kappa|\zeta-vR|^2/2$. Translating and scaling the integral of $\kappa^d e^{-\kappa|\zeta-vR|^2}$ in $2d$ real coordinates gives $\pi^d$.
