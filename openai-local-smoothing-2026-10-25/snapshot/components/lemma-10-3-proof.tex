Put $g_R(x)=g(Rx)$. Its Fourier support is contained in $cR\le|\xi|\le CR$, and $U(t)g_R(x)=(U(Rt)g)(Rx)$. Apply \ref{ms:thm-1-1} on $1\le t\le2$ to $g_R$ at Sobolev order $\sigma$. The Sobolev multiplier $(1+|\xi|^2)^{\sigma/2}$ costs $O(R^\sigma)$ on this annulus: after insertion of a smooth annular cutoff and division by $R^\sigma$, its dilation to scale one has uniformly bounded derivatives of each fixed order. The change of variables contributes $R^{(n+1)/p}$ in the output and $\|g_R\|_p=R^{-n/p}\|g\|_p$, giving $R^{1/p+\sigma}$. Conjugation gives the negative-time bound. For $|t|\le1$, insert a fixed smooth annular cutoff equal to one on the support of $\widehat g$. The symbols of these cut-off propagators are uniformly smooth and compactly supported, so their kernels have bounded $L^1$ norms uniformly in $t$.
