Let $n\ge2$, $d=n-1$, and choose an orthonormal spatial frame $X=(x,b)\in\mathbb R^d\times\mathbb R$. Put
\[
t=(T+b)/\sqrt2,\qquad s_0=(b-T)/\sqrt2,\qquad \mathsf X=(t,x,s_0).
\]
For a frequency $\xi=(\xi_x,\xi_b)$ with $\xi_b+|\xi|>0$, set
\[
\kappa=(\xi_b+|\xi|)/\sqrt2,\qquad v=\xi_x/\kappa,\qquad
u_{\kappa,v}=c_{\kappa,v}\exp\!\left(\mathrm i\kappa(s_0+v\cdot x-|v|^2t/2)\right),
\]
where $\mathrm i$ is the ordinary Fourier imaginary unit. This is the plane wave of physical phase $X\cdot\xi-T|\xi|$. Let $E_{ab}$ be the elementary $n\times n$ matrix indexed by $0\le a,b\le d$, let
\[
C^t=2E_{00},\qquad C^{x_j}=E_{0j}+E_{j0},\qquad C^{s_0}=-\sum_{j=1}^d E_{jj},
\qquad \mathcal P=\sum_\mu C^\mu\partial_{\mathsf X^\mu},
\]
and put $C(p)=\sum_\mu p_\mu C^\mu$. With $p=\kappa(-|v|^2/2,v,1)$,
\[
-C(p)=\kappa
\begin{pmatrix}|v|^2&-v^{\mathsf t}\\-v&I_d\end{pmatrix},
\qquad -w^{\mathsf t}C(p)w=\kappa|w'-vw_0|^2\quad(w=(w_0,w')\in\mathbb R^n).
\]
The kernel is spanned by $E(v)=(1,v)^{\mathsf t}$, and the entire vector wave
\[
f_{\rm sys}=\sum_{\kappa,v}\kappa^{d/2}E(v)u_{\kappa,v}
\]
solves $\mathcal P f_{\rm sys}=0$. Here the sum ranges over an arbitrary finite set of distinct frequencies $\xi$ with $\xi_b+|\xi|>0$, relabelled by $(\kappa,v)$, and the coefficients $c_{\kappa,v}\in\mathbb C$ are arbitrary. For real states $w_1,w_2\in\mathbb R^n$, let $z=w_1+\mathbf k w_2=(R,\zeta)\in\mathbb C_{\mathbf k}\times\mathbb C_{\mathbf k}^d$, where $\mathbb C_{\mathbf k}=\mathbb R+\mathbf k\mathbb R$ and $\mathbf k^2=-1$. The displacement
\[
q^\mu=\tfrac12\sum_{\ell=1}^2w_\ell^{\mathsf t}C^\mu w_\ell=\tfrac12z^*C^\mu z
\]
multiplies each mode at $\mathsf X-\mathrm i q$ by $e^{p\cdot q}=\exp(-\kappa|\zeta-vR|^2/2)$. For each fixed $R$, the square of $\kappa^{d/2}e^{p\cdot q}$ has integral $\pi^d$ over $\zeta\in\mathbb C_{\mathbf k}^d$ with ordinary Lebesgue measure on its $2d$ real coordinates.
