Fix \(n\ge2\) and \(0<c_0<C_0\), and put \(p_c=2n/(n-1)\).
Let \(\mathbb T^n=\mathbb R^n/(100\mathbb Z)^n\), with Lebesgue
volume on a fundamental cube. Define \(U(T)\) by its multiplier
\(e^{-iT|\xi|}\) on the torus frequencies \((2\pi/100)\mathbb Z^n\).
There is \(\gamma>0\), depending only on \(n,c_0,C_0\), such that
the following holds. Suppose \(0<h\le1\) and a trigonometric
polynomial \(f\) has frequencies \(c_0/h\le|\xi|\le C_0/h\) in
one cap, expressed in an orthonormal frame as
\(\xi=(\xi',\xi_b)\), \(\kappa=(\xi_b+|\xi|)/\sqrt 2>0\), and
\(|\xi'/\kappa|\le\gamma\). Then
\[
 \|U(T)f\|_{L^{p_c}([1,2]\times\mathbb T^n)}
 \le C[\log(2/h)]^{2/p_c}\|f\|_{L^{p_c}(\mathbb T^n)}.
\]
The constant \(C\) depends only on \(n,c_0,C_0\).
