Let \(n\ge2\), \(d=n-1\), \(p=2n/d\), \(q=p/2\), and \(L(R)=\log(2+R)\). With \(Eg(X)=\int_{S^{n-1}}e^{iX\cdot\omega}g(\omega)\,d\sigma(\omega)\) using surface measure, suppose that for some \(A\ge0\) and every ball \(B(z,R)\) of radius \(R\ge2\) and every \(g\in L^p(S^{n-1},d\sigma)\),
\[
 \|Eg\|_{L^p(B(z,R))}\lesssim L(R)^A\|g\|_{L^p(S^{n-1})}.
\]
Let \(0<\delta\le1/2\), let \(\{\omega_j\}\subset S^{n-1}\) be \(\delta\)-separated, and let \(T_j\) be the open \(2\delta\)-neighborhood of \(x_j+[-1/2,1/2]\omega_j\) for arbitrary \(x_j\in\mathbb R^n\). For \(a_j\ge0\),
\[
 \left\|\sum_j a_j\mathbf1_{T_j}\right\|_{L^q(\mathbb R^n)}
 \lesssim L(1/\delta)^{2A}\left(\delta^d\sum_j a_j^q\right)^{1/q}.
\]
