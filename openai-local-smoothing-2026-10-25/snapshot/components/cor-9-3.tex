Let \(n\ge2\), \(p_c=2n/(n-1)\), and write \(a'\) for the Hölder conjugate of \(a\). On \(S^{n-1}\) with surface measure \(d\sigma\), put \(Eg(x)=\int e^{ix\cdot\omega}g(\omega)\,d\sigma(\omega)\). On the truncated cone \(\Sigma=\{(\rho\omega,\rho):1\le\rho\le2,\ \omega\in S^{n-1}\}\) with induced surface measure \(d\Sigma\), put
\[
 E_\Sigma G(x,t)=\int_\Sigma e^{i(x\cdot\xi+t\tau)}G(\xi,\tau)\,d\Sigma(\xi,\tau).
\]
If \(1<a\le\infty\), \(b<\infty\), and \(b>p_c\) and \(b>(n+1)a'/(n-1)\), then the strong bounds
\[
 \|Eg\|_{L^b(\mathbb R^n)}\lesssim_{n,a,b}\|g\|_{L^a(S^{n-1})},\qquad
 \|E_\Sigma G\|_{L^b(\mathbb R^{n+1})}\lesssim_{n,a,b}\|G\|_{L^a(\Sigma)}
\]
hold. For \(b=\infty\) both bounds hold for every \(1\le a\le\infty\).
