For $r$ between $2^k$ and $2^{k+1}$ the symbol is supported in $2^{k-1}\le|\xi|\le2^{k+1}$. Choose a smooth annular symbol $\chi$ equal to one on $1/2\le|\xi|\le2$ and write $Q_k=\chi(2^{-k}D)$.

For $r=2^ks$, $1\le s\le2$, set $g_k(y)=(Q_k f)(2^{-k}y)$. Then $A_{\delta,r}f(2^{-k}y)=A_{\delta,s}g_k(y)$, and the factor $2^{-kn/p}$ in the output norm cancels $2^{kn/p}$ from $\|g_k\|_p$. Lemma~\ref{ms:shell-unit-radius} applies to $g_k$, which has Fourier support in the fixed annulus of $\chi$. The $L^p$ norm of the supremum in this octave is thus bounded by $C\delta^{-\sigma}\|Q_k f\|_p$. The supremum over the octaves is at most their pointwise $\ell^p$ norm. Since $p\ge2$, the Littlewood--Paley square-function inequality \cite{r-32}, Theorem~6.1.2, gives
\[
 \left(\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}\|Q_k f\|_p^p\right)^{1/p}
 \le\left\|\left(\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}|Q_k f|^2\right)^{1/2}\right\|_p
 \lesssim\|f\|_p.
\]
