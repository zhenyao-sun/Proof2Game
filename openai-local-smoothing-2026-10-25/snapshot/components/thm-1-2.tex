Let \(n\ge2\), \(p_c=2n/(n-1)\), \(I=[1,2]\), and
\(\varphi\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\})\). Use the Fourier transform
\(\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R^n}e^{-iX\cdot\xi}f(X)\,dX\), with
inverse factor \((2\pi)^{-n}\). Let \(U(T)\) be the Fourier multiplier
with symbol \(e^{-iT|\xi|}\). For \(N\ge2\), let \(P_N\) be the
Fourier multiplier with symbol \(\varphi(\xi/N)\). Then, for every
complex Schwartz function \(f\),
\[
 \|U(T)P_N f\|_{L^{p_c}(I\times\mathbb R^n)}
 \le C_{n,\varphi}[\log(2+N)]^{2/p_c}\|f\|_{L^{p_c}(\mathbb R^n)}.
\]
