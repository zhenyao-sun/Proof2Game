The global extension bound is \ref{ms:prop-12-global}, and the critical frequency-ball estimate is \ref{ms:prop-12-fresnel}.

For the mixed-norm estimate choose $p_c<a<p$. Since $a>p_c=1+(d+2)/d$, one has $a>(d+2)a'/d$. Thus \ref{ms:prop-12-global} gives $E:L^a(B_d)\to L^a(\mathbb R^{d+1})$. The diagonal case of Lee, Rogers, and Seeger's Corollary~1.2 \cite{r-51} states that a global $L^a\to L^a$ extension estimate for the unit paraboloid patch with $2<a<\infty$ implies, for any compact time interval $I$, $a<p<\infty$, and $p\leq r\leq\infty$,
\[
\|e^{\mathrm it\Delta}f\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^d;L^r(I))}
 \lesssim \|f\|_{B^{d(1-2/p)-2/r}_{p,p}(\mathbb R^d)}.
\]
Apply this with the chosen $a$. Finally, the dyadic $\ell^p$ norm is bounded by the dyadic $\ell^2$ norm pointwise because $p>2$. Spatial Littlewood--Paley theory then gives
\[
\|f\|_{B^s_{p,p}}\leq
 \left\|\left(\sum_{j\geq0}2^{2js}|P_j f|^2\right)^{1/2}\right\|_p
 \lesssim_{d,p,s}\|(1-\Delta)^{s/2}f\|_p.
\]
