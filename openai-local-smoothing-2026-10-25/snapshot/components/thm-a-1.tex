Let $m\ge1$, let $\Lambda\subset\mathbb R^m$ be a full lattice, and let $V\subset\mathbb R^{2m}$ be a bounded smooth domain. Write $\Omega=(\mathbb R^m/\Lambda)\times V$. Write $x_{a0}$ for the periodic coordinates and group the remaining coordinates of $V$ as $(x_{a1},x_{a2})_{a=1}^m$ in the $\mathbf i,\mathbf j$ slots. Set $(e_0,e_1,e_2)=(1,\mathbf i,\mathbf j)$ and $\partial_{a\alpha}=\partial/\partial x_{a\alpha}$. For a real function $v$, the three-slot Hessian is
\[
H_{ab}(v)=\sum_{\alpha,\beta=0}^2e_\alpha\bar e_\beta\,\partial_{a\alpha}\partial_{b\beta}v .
\]
The determinant is the Moore determinant. Let $r\in C^\infty(\overline V)$ be a defining function, negative in $V$, with $\mathrm dr\ne0$ on $\partial V$ and $H(r)>0$ on $\overline\Omega$. Suppose $B_0,g\in C^\infty(\overline\Omega)$ are real and satisfy
\[
 H(B_0)\ge0,\qquad g>\det H(B_0)\quad\text{on }\overline\Omega .
\]
Then there is a unique smooth solution up to the boundary of
\[
 \det H(B_0+u)=g,\qquad H(B_0+u)>0,\qquad u|_{\partial\Omega}=0.
\]
There are constants $b\ge b'>0$ such that $br\le u\le b'r$. For smooth finite-dimensional families of such domains and data, the barrier constants may be chosen locally uniformly, and the solutions depend smoothly on the parameters up to the boundary after a smooth local identification of domains.

The comparison principle also holds on subdomains with the same three-slot operator. If two smooth real potentials $\psi_i$ have positive definite Hessians up to the boundary, $\det H(\psi_1)\ge\det H(\psi_2)$ in the subdomain and $\psi_1\le\psi_2$ on its boundary, then $\psi_1\le\psi_2$ throughout.
