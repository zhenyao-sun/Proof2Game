Let \(n\ge2\), \(d=n-1\), \(I=[1,2]\), and let
\(\varphi\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\})\).
Use the Fourier transform
\(\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R^n} e^{-iX\cdot\xi}f(X)\,dX\)
with inverse factor \((2\pi)^{-n}\). For \(0<h\le1/2\) let
\(U(T)\) and \(P_h\) be the multipliers \(e^{-iT|\xi|}\) and
\(\varphi(h\xi)\), respectively. For \(2\le p\le\infty\), set
\[
 s_+(p)=\max\{0,d/2-n/p\},\qquad
 \beta(p)=\min\{d(1-2/p),2/p\},
\]
with \(1/\infty=0\). For complex Schwartz \(f\) one has
\[
 \|U(T)P_hf\|_{L^p(I\times\mathbb R^n)}
 \le C_{n,p,\varphi}\,h^{-s_+(p)}[\log(2/h)]^{\beta(p)}
                 \|f\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)} .
\]
