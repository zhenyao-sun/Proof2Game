By the rank-one determinant identity of Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-3-2},
\[
 \det D_{\theta u}
 =\theta^n\det D_u+(1-\theta)\theta^{n-1}C(D_u):B_{\rm fib}.
\]
Put $I=\int_{\Omega_z}(-u)C(D_u):B_{\rm fib}\,\mathrm d\zeta\ge0$. Integrating that polynomial in $\theta$ gives $\mathcal F=nX_0/(n+1)-I/[\lambda n(n+1)]$ and hence the upper bound. Homogeneity gives $C(D_u):D_u=n\det D_u$; therefore
\[
 n\lambda X_0-I=\int_{\Omega_z}(-u)C(D_u):H_{\rm fib}(u)\,\mathrm d\zeta
 =\int_{\Omega_z}G_{\rm fib}(u)^*C(D_u)G_{\rm fib}(u)\,\mathrm d\zeta.
\]
In the last equality the encoded gradient has entries $G_{{\rm fib},j}(u)=\partial_{j0}u+\mathbf i\partial_{j1}u+\mathbf j\partial_{j2}u$. The real form of $C(D_u)$ in these slots represents the quaternionic quadratic form on this gradient. Its real columns are divergence free by Lemma~\ref{ms:lemma-3-3}, and the boundary term vanishes since $u=0$. Substitution gives the identity and the lower bound.
