Let $n\ge2$, $2\le p<\infty$ and $\sigma>\max\{0,(n-1)/2-n/p\}$. For $0<\delta\le1/8$ and $\psi\in C_c^\infty((1/2,2))$, define $A_{\delta,r}$ for $r>0$ by the Fourier symbol $\psi((1-|\xi|/r)/\delta)$. Then for Schwartz $f$,
\[
\left\|\sup_{r>0}|A_{\delta,r}f|\right\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)}
\le C\delta^{-\sigma}\|f\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^n)}.
\]
The constant is uniform for $\psi$ in bounded subsets of $C_c^\infty((1/2,2))$.
